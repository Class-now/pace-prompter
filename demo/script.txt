Good afternoon, and thank you for having me.
I want to talk about the one minute before you speak.
Your hands are cold, your heart is loud, and your first sentence comes out too fast.
Most of us were told to just slow down.
But nobody tells you where to slow down.
So I built a prompter that listens to your pulse and your breathing.
When your body runs ahead of your words, it waits for one breath.
Then it gives you the next line.
After the run, it shows where the pauses belong.
Thank you.
